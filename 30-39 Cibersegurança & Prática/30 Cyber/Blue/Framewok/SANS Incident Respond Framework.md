## Preparation
Defining policies, assembling the team, and deploying tools to prepare for potential threats

## Identification
Detecting, validating, and analyzing unusual activity to determine if an incident has occurred.
## Containment
Limiting the damage and preventing the attack from spreading further into the network.
## Eradication
Identifying and removing the root cause of the threat, such as deleting malware or disabling compromised accounts.
## Recovery
Restoring system to normal operation, validating security, and confirming functionality.
## Lessons Learned
Conducting a post-incident review to analyze the response and improve future security measures.
